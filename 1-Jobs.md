I currently work as a network engineer at a company. My main job is to design, configure, and maintain the company's network system, including routers, switches, and firewalls. I also monitor the network to make sure it runs smoothly and securely.

In a typical week, I usually start on Monday by checking the network status and reviewing any issues from the weekend. From Tuesday to Thursday, I mostly work on configuring devices, troubleshooting connection problems, and upgrading the system. On Friday, I update the documentation and report our progress.

As for who I work with, I collaborate closely with other engineers on my team, such as system administrators and security specialists. I also work with the IT helpdesk, who send me problems from users, and sometimes with vendors or customers when we need to set up a new connection.

What I enjoy most is that every day brings a new challenge, so I'm always learning something new. Overall, it's a busy but rewarding job.
